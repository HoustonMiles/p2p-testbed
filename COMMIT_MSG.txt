Relay step 5a: TURN relay in the testbed, and a TURN client

The relay baseline's first half: the relay itself, and the client code a
peer will use to reach it (step 5b wires it into punch).

- testbed: a relay service, coturn as a TURN relay (RFC 8656) at the
  reserved 198.18.0.102, from the same image as stun but its own
  container. Long-term credentials testbed / testbed, realm p2p-testbed;
  relayed ports 49152-65535; UDP only. RELAY_DELAY_MS (default 0) delays
  what the relay sends, to model a relay out of the way (2 x the delay on
  a relayed round trip); a new experiment setting that is kept out of
  older configurations' IDs (all 120 unchanged).
- p2p/internal/stun: the message format behind the Binding helpers
  (Message, Encode, Decode, XOR addresses) and MESSAGE-INTEGRITY
  (AppendIntegrity, CheckIntegrity), which TURN builds on.
- p2p/internal/turn: the client side, socket-free like stun: Allocate with
  the long-term 401 -> realm/nonce -> signed retry, stale nonce (438),
  CreatePermission, ChannelBind, Refresh, ChannelData framing.
  Success responses must carry our MESSAGE-INTEGRITY; a 401 to signed
  credentials does not retry. No new dependencies.
- p2prunner: smoke checks that a peer reaches the relay through its NAT;
  preflight lists the relay; the ARP-table check counts it.
- NAT checks (verify and the start of each configuration) test a peer once
  more when its test got no reply at all ("none"), instead of restarting
  the whole configuration; a wrong answer still fails at once.
- docs: testbed.md (relay, RELAY_DELAY_MS, address), design.md, testing.md,
  results.md (also the mesh_join coast results: about 425 ms join time in
  every configuration, six round trips).

Tests: MESSAGE-INTEGRITY against the RFC 5769 sample request; the TURN
login, forged and wrong-credential replies, stale nonce, channel binding
and ChannelData against a fake server; a fuzz target for every reply;
a live relay-to-relay test against coturn (TURN_SERVER=...), run from the
host and from behind a port-restricted NAT; the relay smoke check; the
NAT-check retry (mutation-checked).
