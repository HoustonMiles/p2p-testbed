Relay step 5b: punch -relay, every link through the TURN relay

The relayed baseline in the peer: the same steps and columns as the punched
modes, so raw, direct and relayed runs line up.

- punch -relay (with -relay-server, -relay-user, -relay-pass): the peer
  allocates a relayed address before it registers at the rendezvous; each
  link binds a TURN channel to its member's relayed address (in place of
  the punch), meets it at the connected barrier through the relay, and
  sends only to that address, so both IPs stay hidden. Data travels as
  ChannelData. The allocation is released at the end.
- With -direct too: the Noise handshake runs through the relay and every
  packet after it is sealed with the session, so the relay forwards only
  ciphertext (no hopping). A link opens sealed packets as soon as its
  handshake is done and seals its own after the member's barrier, which
  the member sends only once its own handshake is done.
- wire: Register and PeerInfo carry an optional relayed address after the
  key (Extras; the length says which is present).
- rendezvous: stores and passes on relayed addresses, and introduces a
  member that registered one by it alone: in relayed mode no member
  learns another's IP, not even from the rendezvous.
- Columns: relayed, relay_addr, relay_ms, peer_relay_addr, relay_bind_ms;
  rx_ip_mbit also counts ChannelData's header.
- mesh trial: a "relay" outcome; with -relay in args every pair is
  predicted to connect.
- runner: smoke and verify refuse to run while containers of another
  stack run too (the orphan mix after an experiment); run says which
  configuration it leaves running and how to go back.

Testbed (no netem): two symmetric NATs, which cannot punch, connected
through coturn with Noise: 20/20 pings, 20 Mbit/s, no loss, each side
knowing only the other's relayed address. 8 peers of every NAT type:
28/28 pairs through the relay (time to full mesh 0.46 s), against 15/28
hop-connected in direct mode; both 100% as predicted.

Tests: loopback meshes through a fake TURN relay, raw and Noise, with
the relay unable to read a single Data packet under -direct
(mutation-checked); a member not in relayed mode; relayed addresses
through the rendezvous, own address never handed out; the wire tail;
the relay outcome; the two-stack check. Race suite clean.
